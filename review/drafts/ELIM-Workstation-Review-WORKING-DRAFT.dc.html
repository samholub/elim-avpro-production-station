The decision

Bianca and I recommend building the rolling production workstation and the Wing case. The one-time cost is about $4,600 ($4,641.84).

Why this matters

Our production rig is deployed once or twice a week at different venues. We set it up and strike it under time pressure.

What we'd build

A rolling workstation case holds the video, network and control gear, with two monitors on the flip-up lid. Bay A is the active system. Bay B is storage and camera charging.

The Wing sits beside it in the ProX BXWING hydraulic flip case (the Wing case), joined by five short cables. Every field cable and those five plug into one labeled, color-coded panel on the back.

The design is decided.

Risks

The rollout is staged, and that's decided. The owned gear moves into the cases in stages, starting from a setup closest to today's, and each stage is proved before the next.

Weather is an accepted risk, because we often set up outside with no cover. The plan for it is still open. The plan is a fitted rain cover or poly sheet and clamps in the case, with a two-minute cover-and-kill routine. I'll have it in place before the first live deploy.

Who decides

You give the final sign-off, and Bianca makes the go/no-go decision on ordering. I design and lead the build. Matt quotes the cases and freight. This approval chain is decided.

Sunny, do you approve a one-time spend of about $4,600 ($4,641.84)? Bianca will release the order once Matt's quote comes in under the ceiling she and I agree on before we meet.

Supporting detail

What goes where Decided design locked, DESIGN section 1

Bay A
The active system: fan, Resi, the recorder and the new 8-port PoE Dante satellite switch, the LED wall transcoder, production switch and router, cable management, reserve blanks, the UPS-backed strip and the UPS. Every network jumper stays in Bay A. Its 4U top is left open, and the Hollyland RX mounts on top of the flip-up lid.
Bay B
Storage and utility power: the metered CPPDU511 at the front (drawer lights) and the RS1215-RA at the rear (camera chargers), three U's left open, and two 4U drawers with heavier items low. No signal. Its 4U top is left open. All cabling is stored with the two cases.
Wing case
The ProX BXWING hydraulic flip case (the Wing case) holds the Wing, the owned 5-port TL-SG1005P Dante switch and the ProTools Mac. The ProTools Mac has USB-C to the Wing for recording, plus a Dante patch, and handles both the live event mix and the stream mix. The case sits beside the AVPRO, joined by five short cables.
Rear panel
Three Neutrik D-series plates on the Bay A rear rails: video, data/audio/power, and a spare row. It is the one face anyone setting up touches. Field cables land here; short jumpers run to the gear.
